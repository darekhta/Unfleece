Questions

Frequently asked

Is Merge PDF really free?
Yes — free, forever, no account and no trial that bites. There's no server bill to recover because nothing is uploaded.
Are my files uploaded anywhere?
No. Your browser reads the files into memory and merges them on your device. Open the Network tab and watch — you'll see zero upload requests.
Is there a file-size or page limit?
No hard limit. The only ceiling is your browser's memory, so very large files may be slow. We warn you before that becomes a problem.
Does it work offline?
Once the page has loaded, yes. You can turn off Wi-Fi and merging still works — the strongest proof that nothing leaves your device.
